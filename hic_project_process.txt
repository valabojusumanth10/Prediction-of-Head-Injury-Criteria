STEP 4 – DATA PREPROCESSING

System automatically processes the uploaded dataset.

Operations: • Remove noise from data • Normalize numeric values •
Convert dataset into machine learning format

Example processed values: Normalized Impact Speed: 0.72 Normalized Hood
Stiffness: 0.64 Normalized Penetration Depth: 0.58

  ------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 5 – FEATURE EXTRACTION
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  System extracts important features from dataset.

  Examples: • Material Strength Index • Impact Energy • Hood
  Deformation Ratio • Average Penetration Depth
  ------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 6 – TRAIN DEEP NEURAL NETWORK MODEL

Navigate to: /model/train

Training parameters example: Epochs: 50 Batch Size: 32 Learning Rate:
0.001

Example training output:

Epoch 1 - Loss: 0.56 Epoch 10 - Loss: 0.28 Epoch 25 - Loss: 0.12 Epoch
50 - Loss: 0.05

  ------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 7 – MODEL EVALUATION
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  Evaluation metrics:

  Mean Squared Error (MSE) Correlation Score Prediction
  Accuracy

  Example results:

  MSE: 0.021 Accuracy: 94.3% Correlation: 0.92
  ------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 8 – PREDICT HIC FOR NEW DESIGN

Navigate to: /hic/predict

Input example:

Hood Thickness: 1.4 Hood Stiffness: 390 Material Density: 2700 Youngs
Modulus: 70 Impact Speed: 36 Impact Angle: 47 Hood Mass: 13 Penetration
Depth: 8.1 Energy Absorption: 140

Click Predict HIC.

  ------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 9 – PREDICTION RESULT
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  Example output:

  Predicted HIC Value: 705

  Interpretation:

  HIC < 650 → Low injury risk 650–800 → Moderate injury risk >
  800 → High injury risk

  Example result: Safety Level: Moderate Injury Risk
